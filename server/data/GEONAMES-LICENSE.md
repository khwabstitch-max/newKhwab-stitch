# GeoNames India postal-code coordinates

Source: [GeoNames India postal-code download](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/IN.zip), retrieved 20 November 2026.

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Credit: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). [Source documentation](https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/readme.txt).

Modified for this application: retain valid six-digit Indian PINs with plausible Indian coordinates; average the listed post-office coordinates within each PIN and round to six decimals. These are approximate geographic PIN-area centroids, not road distances or courier rate zones. Missing PINs remain unresolved.
