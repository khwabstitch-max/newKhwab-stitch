# Customer-flow walkthrough

> **Historical document — superseded 2026-11-10.** Use [project status](project-status.md), [production readiness](production-readiness.md), the [current admin guide](admin-guide.md) and [customer workflow](customer-workflow.md) for the final implementation and evidence. **Every sleeve length adds zero:** earlier standalone length fees or combined per-length pricing instructions no longer apply. Submitted orders and accepted quotes remain preserved. Test counts, command results, provider limits and deployment/data statements below describe their dated runs, including earlier work on the same date; they are not current verification or launch approval.

Khwab Stitch stitches or alters your fabric; catalogue photographs describe design inspiration and stitching services. Starting prices are estimates until the atelier reviews the request and sends a final quote.

## Create a request

1. Explore designs while signed out. Choose a catalogue design and select **Same as shown** to preserve its included details and go directly to measurements. Select **Customize this design** to change only the parts you choose. Reset an individual part to its original design when needed.
2. For your own reference, sign in before uploading a private photo. A local file selected in a browser is not guaranteed to survive a full-page sign-in redirect; complete the authenticated upload first. For each part, choose your reference or a Khwab Stitch option independently. Choose the relevant uploaded reference when there is more than one. Unclear components remain subject to tailor review.
3. Enter measurements in the dedicated step, using inches or centimetres and identifying body versus garment measurements. Select and explicitly confirm a saved measurement profile if appropriate. Request help if unsure. Changing a design may require confirming affected measurements; unrelated values stay with the draft.
4. Describe the fabric and choose how it reaches the atelier and how the finished outfit reaches you. Your own courier is paid directly by you. Business-arranged transport requires an address and serviceability review; it may have a pending quote. Choosing a transport option does not book a courier.
5. Review the editable sections. Known charges and pending work are shown separately. Use Edit, Save changes or Cancel changes without losing unrelated sections. A reference design requires feasibility and final-price confirmation; exact reproduction is not guaranteed.
6. Acknowledge the applicable service terms and submit the request explicitly. If sign-in is needed, return to the restored review and submit yourself. A successful login does not submit a request. Wait for the actual saved request number; failed submissions retain the draft and safe retries reuse the request key.

Signed-out ordinary design choices are recoverable on the current browser/device for 30 days. Measurements, addresses, contact details, notes and private reference IDs are kept only in the current tab's session storage; closing that session can require re-entering those details/re-uploading references. They are excluded from persistent local storage. Authenticated cloud drafts are private to the account and appear in Saved drafts. The interface distinguishes saving, saved and failed states. A conflict from another tab requires reviewing/reloading rather than silently overwriting newer work. Do not assume an unfinished local upload has been saved.

Catalogue search includes English/Hindi names and design codes. Use **Load more catalogue designs** or **Load more designs** to browse further choices; selections and entered details stay with the draft. Component search filters the designs already loaded, so load additional batches to find further matches. The account lists have page controls; changing a list page preserves an open form or chosen completed order. The server confirms review eligibility and prevents duplicate reviews.

## Separate customization sections

For **Customize this design**, choose only the sections you want to change: sleeve length, sleeve design, front neck, back neck and extra add-ons. Fit remains available. **Same as shown** skips these customization steps and preserves the catalogue's original included details.

Sleeve length cards describe a style preference. They do not provide a fixed size: enter the exact length in inches/cm during measurements or request assistance. Sleeveless hides the sleeve-design step, removes its fee and sleeve measurements, and keeps the previous sleeve choice/values saved for switching back. Only configured compatible sleeve/length combinations are offered; missing combinations need tailor review. Changing a front neck never silently changes the back neck.

Each part can keep the original, use a published option, follow a protected uploaded reference or request master-tailor guidance. References/help remain price-after-review. Use the help link to send an asynchronous question through the existing consented support flow.

Extra add-ons are optional and individually removable, including a saved addition that has become unavailable. Included catalogue additions have no duplicate surcharge. Each addition explains whether its price covers stitching labor, specified supplied materials, or requires review; fabric/lining/lace are not implied to be supplied. Unavailable future crafts are not offered as active finishing additions.

Final review has an Edit action for all five custom sections. Save changes or Cancel changes preserves unrelated measurements, notes, references and logistics. A changed sleeve/neck can require reconfirming affected numeric measurements without erasing them. Combined sleeve pricing uses one explicit configured adjustment rather than adding two charges.

## Review, track and get help

The account area contains your orders, drafts, measurements, profile/addresses, support, reviews and Work with us. Editing a saved address or measurement profile changes future choices; it does not rewrite a submitted order. Request order-specific changes through support so staff can review design, measurement and price consequences.

The atelier reviews the request, sends the final quote and confirms timing. Accept only the current quote. If it changes before acceptance, review the new version. Tracking shows actual authorized updates and recorded dates, not a percentage generated by elapsed time. A courier link or estimated completion date appears only when staff supply it. Transport and stitching status are distinct.

Use **Need Help from a Master Tailor?** or the support section to send an asynchronous question linked to the relevant step, draft or order. Choose whether to share the offered design context; measurements and addresses are not automatically attached. Only implemented contact methods are available. A reply or callback is not instant or guaranteed. Your draft remains available while you wait.

For a complaint, select the relevant order/category, describe the concern and optionally attach private photos. Follow its ticket conversation and status; an automated receipt is different from a human response and a resolution. Configured support hours govern any stated first-response target. A correction/transport plan may need additional time. A resolved request can be reopened where the workflow permits it.

After a delivered order, submit one honest rating/review and choose whether it may be displayed publicly. Public reviews are selected/moderated; staff do not rewrite your rating or wording. Work with us opens a private application; it does not automatically provide worker access or promise work/payment.

## Sign-in and recovery

Use email/password or Google when that method is enabled by the business. Existing email/password customers link a matching Google account from Account and security after proving their current password; a matching email alone never takes ownership of an account. Google-only users manage their Google password through Google. When configured by the business, application email verification and forgot-password use emailed one-use links. Application password change/reset ends existing sessions and requires signing in again. Keep the link private and request a fresh one when it expires. If recovery is unavailable, contact the atelier through an actually configured channel.

The responsibility statement concerns errors against confirmed design/measurements. Customer-requested changes, fabric/design limitations and correction transport follow the business's published policy. Alteration margin is subject to fabric/design inspection; it is not extra body ease or a universal guarantee.
